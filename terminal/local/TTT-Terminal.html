Impact rules — HIGH only when the market actually reacts

News, posts and data releases are HIGH only if, within the window after they hit, at least N of the listed futures moved by the futures threshold, or a Mag 10 stock named in the story moved by the stock threshold. Important stories without a move are LOW. Economic calendar shows US events only: Forex Factory red = HIGH, orange = INTERMEDIATE, other US events only if the futures moved.

CONNECT LIVE DATA — one-time, about 5 minutes, free

Already connected — your relay just needs the latest code (it adds Trump's Truth Social posts). Open your ttt-proxy worker in Cloudflare → Edit code, then repeat step 3 below (replace all the code, Deploy) and click Test & Save in step 4. Your address stays the same.

Browsers don't let a web page read Yahoo Finance, Nasdaq or news feeds directly, and the free public relays have shut down. A free Cloudflare Worker relays the data for you. It runs in Cloudflare's cloud, so nothing is installed or run on your PC. Only the market and news sites the terminal uses are allowed through.

  1. Create a free Cloudflare account: dash.cloudflare.com/sign-up (no credit card needed).
  2. Create a Worker: in the left menu open Workers & Pages (under Compute), then click Create → Start with Hello World! → name it ttt-proxy → Deploy.
  3. Paste the relay code: click Edit code, select everything in the editor (Ctrl+A) and delete it, then paste this code and click Deploy.
  4. Connect: copy your worker's address from the Cloudflare page (it ends in .workers.dev), paste it here and click Test & Save.

Free plan: 100,000 requests a day. The terminal uses roughly 30 per refresh, so auto-refresh every minute all day is well within it.
